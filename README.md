# Études

An étude is a study a painter makes to learn one thing: a hand, a fold of cloth, a particular light. It stays in the studio. Musicians use the same word for a piece written to train a skill. The work is finished enough to keep, and it exists so the next one can be better.

This repository is that studio. It is the collection of projects I made while learning machine learning. Each notebook is one étude: one table, one question, a few models, and a record of what the numbers meant.

## Studies

The studies are numbered in the order they were made.

| # | Study | Notebook | What one row is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Iris | [`01_iris/iris_classification.ipynb`](01_iris/iris_classification.ipynb) | One flower, four measurements, one of three species. |
| 02 | Heart disease | [`02_heart_disease/heart_disease.ipynb`](02_heart_disease/heart_disease.ipynb) | One patient, and the Cleveland diagnosis. |
| 03 | Wine quality | [`03_wine_quality/wine_quality.ipynb`](03_wine_quality/wine_quality.ipynb) | One wine, its lab measurements, and a sensory score. |
| 04 | Titanic | [`04_titanic/titanic.ipynb`](04_titanic/titanic.ipynb) | One passenger, and whether they survived. |
| 05 | House prices | [`05_house_price/house_price.ipynb`](05_house_price/house_price.ipynb) | One house, and its sale price. |
| 06 | California home value | [`06_california_home_value/california_home_value.ipynb`](06_california_home_value/california_home_value.ipynb) | One census block group, and its median home value. |
| 07 | Mushroom | [`07_mushroom/mushroom.ipynb`](07_mushroom/mushroom.ipynb) | One mushroom, and whether it is edible or poisonous. |
| 08 | Breast cancer | [`08_breast_cancer/breast_cancer.ipynb`](08_breast_cancer/breast_cancer.ipynb) | One breast mass, and whether it is malignant or benign. |
| 09 | Bank marketing | [`09_bank_marketing/bank_marketing.ipynb`](09_bank_marketing/bank_marketing.ipynb) | One client called in a campaign, and whether they subscribed to a term deposit. |
| 10 | Adult income | [`10_adult/adult.ipynb`](10_adult/adult.ipynb) | One adult from the 1994 US Census, and whether they earn more than $50K a year. |

The same gesture runs through all of them. [`shared_arc.md`](shared_arc.md) writes it down: load the table, look at the target, hold out a fifth of the rows, fit a simple baseline, then change one thing at a time.
